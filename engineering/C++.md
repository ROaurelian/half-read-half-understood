Statically typed language (variables do not change type).
Low level and performance oriented.

|double|8|-1.7e308 to 1.7e308|
|long double|8|-3.4e932 to 3.4e932|
|char|1|0 to 255|

While storing float its important to add the suffix F or f to the number (otherwise it'll be treated as a double).
Similarly, while storing long add the suffix L or l to the number.
To initialize variables use the assign operator `=` or braces `{}`.
