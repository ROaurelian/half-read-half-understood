Abstraction level between the hardware and software.
Does memory management, I/O, multitasking (core functionality).
Responds to system calls (syscalls) made by the [[OS]].
The most popular is the Linux kernel due to its efficiency, versatility, speed and open source nature.
Microsoft used the NT Kernel and Apple the XNU Kernel.

### Types
- Monolithic
- Microkernel
- Exokernel
- Nanokernel
- Hybrid
